# Power play, cards and referees

Source: hoqueipatins.pt play-by-play (2021/22 onwards). A power play (PP) starts with a blue card (2 min) or red card (4 min). From 2025/26 the site marks PP start/end; before that, PP windows are inferred from card times. Goals within the window, including the direct free hit/penalty that often comes with a blue card, count as PP goals. Times are to the minute, so a few goals in the same minute as a card may be misassigned. A team can score more than once in one power play, so 'goals per PP' can exceed 1. Team tables include play-off games.
